Balance the Books

Five transactions are waiting to be posted.

Each journal entry is locked behind one question about the BA211Z syllabus. Post all five, and make sure your trial balance balances.

  • A correct answer posts both sides of the entry, the debit and the credit.
  • A wrong answer posts only the debit, so your trial balance goes out of balance.
  • You get one correcting entry for each mistake: answer a new question to post the missing credit. If you miss the correcting entry too, that entry stays unbalanced.
  • Finish with total debits equal to total credits to close the books and earn your completion code.
  • Play as many times as you like. Each round draws new questions and new amounts.
